SPACE SCIENCE IN ETHIOPIA

Ethiopian Space Science Society (ESSS)

Founded in 2004 with 47 founding members. A non-profit for professionals and others interested in astronomy, astrophysics and space technology, based in Addis Ababa.

MISSION

Space science for everyone

  • Make space science part of public life.
  • Help people understand space science and its benefits.
  • Inspire innovation, national pride and skills for the future.
VISION & GOALS

A strong scientific culture

A highly developed scientific culture helps Ethiopia reap the benefits of space science.

Near-term: grow participation

Build awareness, membership and education.

Long-term: places to discover

Lalibela Observatory and Ethiopia's first planetarium.

These are goals, not completed facilities.

MILESTONES

From a society to a space program

2012ESSS established the Entoto Observatory Research Center.
2016Government created the Ethiopian Space Science and Technology Institute.
2018National space policy formulated.
2019ETRSS-1, Ethiopia's first satellite, launched.
